
# AI Toolkit for UMKM Indonesia

Free and open-source AI resources to help Indonesian small businesses improve branding, marketing, and productivity.

## About This Project

AI Toolkit for UMKM Indonesia is a community-driven open-source project that provides practical AI guides, resources, templates, and workflows for Indonesian small businesses.

Our mission is to make artificial intelligence more accessible and useful for local entrepreneurs, freelancers, and creative professionals.

## What You Can Find Here

- AI tools and practical tutorials
- Branding and marketing resources
- Social media content planning templates
- Copywriting prompts for businesses
- AI workflows for daily business operations
- Educational resources for beginners

## Who Is This For?

- Small business owners (UMKM)
- Freelancers
- Social media specialists
- Content creators
- Digital marketers
- Anyone learning to use AI for business

## Project Goals

1. Make AI easier to understand and use.
2. Help UMKM improve their digital presence.
3. Provide free and accessible learning resources.
4. Encourage collaboration and knowledge sharing.
5. Build an open community around AI education in Indonesia.

## Language

Our primary language is Bahasa Indonesia.

English documentation may be added to help reach a wider community.
