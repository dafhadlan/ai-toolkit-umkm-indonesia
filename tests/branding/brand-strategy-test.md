# Brand Strategy Generator — Test Report

## 1. Test Information

- Project: AI Toolkit for UMKM Indonesia
- Prompt: UMKM Brand Strategy Generator
- AI Platform: ChatGPT
- Test Type: Functional and Output Quality Testing
- Test Status: Successfully Executed
- Test Date: November 18, 2026

## 2. Test Objective

Evaluate whether the UMKM Brand Strategy Generator can produce relevant, practical, and structured branding recommendations for Indonesian small businesses.

## 3. Test Business Profile

This test uses simulated business information.

| Information | Details |
|---|---|
| Business Name | Rendang Uni Sari |
| Business Category | Traditional Minangkabau Food |
| Product | Packaged Rendang |
| Location | Padang, West Sumatra |
| Target Customers | Adults aged 25–45 |
| Price Range | IDR 45,000–85,000 |
| Main Strength | Traditional family recipe |
| Main Challenge | Inconsistent branding |

## 4. Test Results

The prompt successfully generated the following branding recommendations:

### Brand Identity

- Brand Origin: Padang, West Sumatra
- Target Market: Adults aged 25–45
- Positioning: Packaged rendang with authentic Minangkabau family flavors
- Proposed USP: Traditional family recipe
- Brand Personality: Warm, traditional, trustworthy, simple, and high-quality
- Brand Voice: Friendly, warm, and persuasive
- Main Message: Cita Rasa Minang, Warisan Resep Keluarga
- Tagline: Rasa Minang, Rasa di Hati
- Visual Direction: Modern traditional

### Content Strategy

The AI recommended five Instagram content pillars:

| Content Pillar | Recommended Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Product Showcase | 25% |
| Brand Story | 25% |
| Educational | 20% |
| Social Proof | 15% |
| Sales & Promotion | 15% |

### Customer Experience

The AI also recommended improving the customer journey, including Instagram discovery, WhatsApp inquiries, purchasing, delivery, customer feedback, and repeat purchases.

### Business Information Validation

The AI identified several missing business details:

- Production location
- Product weight
- Product shelf life
- Halal certification and business permits
- Direct competitors
- Existing visual identity

## 5. Strengths Identified

1. The prompt successfully executed its branding analysis.
2. Recommendations were relevant to the Indonesian food business context.
3. The AI generated practical Instagram content pillars.
4. The AI identified missing business information.
5. The output included actionable branding recommendations.

## 6. Issues Identified

The proposed USP requires further validation.

The AI initially acknowledged that a family recipe does not automatically represent a proven competitive advantage. However, it later presented the family recipe as the proposed USP.

The distinction between a proposed USP and a validated USP needs improvement.

The recommended Instagram content percentages are strategic suggestions, not percentages validated through actual account performance data.

## 7. Recommended Improvements

- Distinguish proposed USP from validated USP.
- Require customer and competitor validation before confirming brand differentiation.
- Clearly label assumptions and recommendations.
- Explain that content distribution percentages are initial recommendations.
- Improve consistency between different sections of the final output.

## 8. Conclusion

The UMKM Brand Strategy Generator successfully completed its first functional test using ChatGPT.

The prompt demonstrated potential for helping Indonesian small businesses develop initial branding strategies.

However, further refinement and testing with additional business cases are necessary before considering the prompt fully validated.

Test classification: Successfully Executed — Improvement Required.
