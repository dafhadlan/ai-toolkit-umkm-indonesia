## How to Use These Prompts

1. Choose a prompt category.
2. Copy the prompt you need.
3. Open your preferred AI assistant.
4. Replace the example information with your business details.
5. Review and refine the AI-generated result.

## Prompt Guidelines

All prompts should:
- Use clear and understandable language.
- Be practical for Indonesian businesses.
- Include relevant business context.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Allow users to customize the results.
