
# UMKM Brand Strategy Generator

## Purpose

This prompt helps Indonesian small business owners develop a clear and practical brand strategy using AI.

## Instructions

Act as an experienced brand strategist specializing in Indonesian small businesses (UMKM).

Help the business owner develop a structured brand strategy based on the information provided.

Do not invent business facts. If important information is missing, ask relevant questions before making recommendations.

## Business Information

- Business name: [Enter business name]
- Business category: [Enter business category]
- Products or services: [Describe products or services]
- Business location: [Enter city or region]
- Target customers: [Describe target customers]
- Price range: [Enter price range]
- Main competitors: [Enter competitors]
- Business strengths: [Describe business strengths]
- Brand challenges: [Describe current challenges]

## Expected Output

### 1. Brand Overview
Explain the business concept, value, and market opportunity.

### 2. Target Audience
Identify the primary audience, customer needs, problems, and purchasing motivations.

### 3. Brand Positioning
Develop a clear market positioning statement.

### 4. Unique Selling Proposition
Identify what makes the business different from competitors.

### 5. Brand Personality
Define five relevant brand personality traits and explain each one.

### 6. Brand Voice
Recommend the communication style, language, and tone.

### 7. Brand Messaging
Create:
- One main brand message.
- Three supporting messages.
- One short brand tagline.

### 8. Strategic Recommendations
Provide five practical branding actions suitable for the business.

## Quality Guidelines

- Use clear Bahasa Indonesia.
- Avoid generic marketing advice.
- Make recommendations relevant to Indonesian consumers.
- Explain the reasoning behind important recommendations.
- Prioritize practical and affordable strategies.
- Clearly distinguish facts from assumptions.

## Final Objective

Create a brand strategy that is clear, relevant, realistic, and actionable for an Indonesian small business.

## USP Validation Rules

When developing a Unique Selling Proposition (USP):

1. Separate proposed USP from validated USP.
2. Do not automatically treat a family recipe, product feature, or business history as a proven competitive advantage.
3. Explain what makes the proposed USP potentially different.
4. Identify what customer research or competitor analysis is needed to validate it.
5. If evidence is insufficient, clearly label the USP as a preliminary hypothesis.
6. Ensure all USP recommendations remain consistent throughout the entire report.

Always distinguish:
- Confirmed business facts.
- Proposed brand advantages.
- Unvalidated assumptions.

Never present an unvalidated USP as a proven market advantage.
