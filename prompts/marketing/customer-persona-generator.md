Help Indonesian small businesses understand their potential customers and develop practical customer personas for marketing strategies.

## Expected Output

### 1. Customer Segmentation

Identify relevant customer segments based on:
- Customer needs.
- Purchasing behavior.
- Location.
- Budget.
- Product preferences.
- Customer problems.

### 2. Primary Customer Persona

Create a detailed customer profile including:
- Persona name.
- General profile.
- Needs and expectations.
- Purchasing motivations.
- Main challenges.
- Product selection criteria.

Clearly label hypothetical details.

### 3. Customer Pain Points

Identify:
- Main customer problems.
- Unmet needs.
- Common purchasing objections.
- Factors that influence buying decisions.

### 4. Customer Journey

Describe:
- Awareness stage.
- Consideration stage.
- Purchase stage.
- Retention stage.
- Recommendation stage.

### 5. Marketing Communication Strategy

Recommend:
- Relevant messaging.
- Suitable content topics.
- Communication tone.
- Marketing channels.
- Appropriate calls to action.

### 6. Customer Engagement Ideas

Provide five practical ways to build trust and engagement with the target audience.

### 7. Strategic Recommendations

Recommend actionable marketing improvements based on the customer persona.

## Quality Guidelines

- Use clear Bahasa Indonesia.
- Consider Indonesian consumer behavior.
- Distinguish research findings from assumptions.
- Avoid unsupported demographic claims.
- Prioritize actionable recommendations.
- Do not stereotype customers.

## Important Instructions

If customer research data is unavailable, explicitly state that the personas are initial hypotheses that require validation through interviews, surveys, or actual customer behavior.

Ask clarification questions when essential business information is missing.

## Final Objective

Help Indonesian UMKM understand their customers and develop more relevant, effective, and customer-focused marketing strategies.
