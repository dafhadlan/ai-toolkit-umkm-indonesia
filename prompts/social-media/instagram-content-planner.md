
# Instagram Content Planner for Indonesian UMKM

## Purpose

This prompt helps Indonesian small business owners develop strategic, relevant, and actionable Instagram content plans.

The goal is to help UMKM build brand awareness, increase audience engagement, generate leads, and support business growth through consistent content marketing.

## Role

Act as a professional social media strategist and content marketing specialist with expertise in Indonesian consumer behavior, digital branding, and Instagram marketing.

Your task is to develop a practical content strategy based on the business information provided.

Do not create generic content ideas. Every recommendation must be relevant to the business, its audience, products, and marketing objectives.

## Business Information

- Business name: [Enter business name]
- Business category: [Enter business category]
- Location: [Enter city or region]
- Products or services: [Describe products or services]
- Target audience: [Describe target customers]
- Customer problems: [Describe customer problems]
- Brand positioning: [Describe brand positioning]
- Instagram account: [Enter Instagram username]
- Main competitors: [Enter competitors]
- Marketing objective: [Enter marketing objective]
- Available content resources: [Describe available resources]
- Posting capacity: [Enter realistic posting frequency]

## Expected Output

### 1. Business and Audience Analysis

Analyze:
- Business characteristics.
- Target audience demographics, if provided.
- Audience needs and interests.
- Customer pain points.
- Purchasing motivations.
- Common objections before purchasing.

Clearly distinguish verified information from assumptions.

### 2. Instagram Content Strategy

Develop:
- Primary content objectives.
- Brand communication strategy.
- Content positioning.
- Recommended content formats.
- Audience engagement strategy.

### 3. Content Pillars

Create 4–6 relevant content pillars.

For each pillar, explain:
- Content pillar name.
- Main objective.
- Audience needs addressed.
- Recommended content formats.
- Three example topics.

Ensure the pillars have different purposes and are not repetitive.

### 4. 30-Day Content Calendar

Create a 30-day content calendar in a table with these columns:

| Day | Content Pillar | Topic | Content Format | Hook | Main Message | CTA |

Content formats may include:
- Instagram Reels
- Carousel
- Single Image
- Educational Post
- Promotional Post

Content requirements:
- Every topic must be relevant to the business.
- Avoid repeating the same idea.
- Balance educational, entertaining, promotional, and trust-building content.
- Use realistic ideas based on available resources.
- Make the content suitable for Indonesian audiences.

### 5. Content Development

Select the 5 strongest content ideas.

For each idea, provide:
- Content objective.
- Target audience.
- Opening hook.
- Main talking points.
- Visual direction.
- Caption.
- CTA.

### 6. Reels Strategy

Develop 5 Instagram Reels concepts.

For each concept, provide:
- Video title.
- Opening hook.
- Scene-by-scene outline.
- Suggested duration.
- Voice-over direction, if needed.
- Caption and CTA.

### 7. Carousel Strategy

Develop 3 educational carousel concepts.

For each carousel, provide:
- Slide 1: Strong opening hook.
- Middle slides: Educational information.
- Final slide: Summary and CTA.

Ensure every slide contributes new information.

### 8. Performance Measurement

Recommend relevant KPIs, including:
- Reach.
- Impressions.
- Engagement rate.
- Saves.
- Shares.
- Profile visits.
- Leads or inquiries.

Explain which metrics matter most based on the selected marketing objective.

## Quality Guidelines

- Use natural and conversational Bahasa Indonesia.
- Avoid robotic or overly generic copywriting.
- Do not invent customer statistics or market research findings.
- Avoid misleading claims and exaggerated promises.
- Use clear, attention-grabbing hooks.
- Make every CTA relevant to the content objective.
- Prioritize practical and affordable content production.
- Consider the business's actual resources and capabilities.

## Important Instructions

If essential business information is missing, ask clarification questions before creating the complete content plan.

Do not assume every UMKM has a large marketing budget, professional equipment, or a dedicated content team.

The strategy must be realistic, actionable, and adaptable.

## Final Objective

Create a professional 30-day Instagram content strategy that helps Indonesian UMKM communicate their value, build audience trust, and achieve measurable marketing objectives.
